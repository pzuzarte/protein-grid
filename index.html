Protein Grid help

A pattern grid and sound editor for the Waldorf Protein. It runs in your browser and sends MIDI to the Protein. Your patterns, sounds and mappings stay on this computer. It needs a current Chrome or Edge on a computer. It's unofficial and not affiliated with Waldorf.

Visits to this page are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies. It never receives your patterns or sounds.

Set up

  1. Connect the Protein to your computer with its USB-C cable, and turn it on.
  2. Allow MIDI access when asked. The MIDI badge turns green when the Protein is found. If it says MIDI blocked, click the icon to the left of the address, set MIDI devices to Allow, then click Allow MIDI.
  3. Click a note name in the grid. You should hear it on the Protein.
  4. Check that Channel at the bottom matches the Protein's MIDI channel in its Settings.

Mapping the Protein's settings

  • The Protein has no fixed MIDI controller numbers for its settings. Each one is mapped on the synth itself, so the Synth tab's sliders do nothing until their setting is mapped.
  • Set up controls walks you through them one at a time, the most useful first. For each one: turn that setting on the Protein, press Shift + Settings, choose Map CC and press the Select encoder, then click Send CC here. The page picks a free controller number for it.
  • Sending also sets that setting to the slider's value, or the middle if it isn't known yet.
  • Skip moves on without mapping, and Done stops. You can carry on later, and the mapped count shows how far you are. Map next to any slider maps just that one, or maps it again.
  • The Protein lists its mappings under Shift + Settings, Mappings, where you can delete one. Forget mapping only forgets it here.
  • The Protein gives no sign over MIDI that a mapping worked, so move the slider afterwards and check that the setting changes. If it doesn't, map it again.
  • Mappings are kept in this browser and in backups. Whether the Protein keeps them through a restart, or for every sound, hasn't been checked yet.

Synth tab

  • Moving a slider sends its setting straight away, even during playback. Values run from 0 to 127, and the Protein spreads that over the setting's own range, so its display shows the exact value. Settings with a middle point show 0 there.
  • A slider shown faint hasn't been set from this page yet. The Protein can't report its settings, so the page doesn't know them until you move them or load a sound.
  • Save sound keeps the sliders' values in this browser under a name, and Load sends them back. Only the mapped settings are sent. These don't replace the Protein's own presets.
  • Vary nudges the known settings a little or a lot, leaving tuning, pan and volume alone. Hear original flips between the sound before you started and the new one.
  • Read from Protein waits for the Protein to send its current sound: on the Protein, press Presets, open the Actions menu and choose Send. Chrome asks for SysEx permission the first time. For now this only receives the dump and lets you save it with Save dump, while the format is worked out. It doesn't change the sliders yet.
  • Mod slots' sources and targets, effect types and their deeper settings, and the arpeggiator and sequencer are set on the Protein.

Working in a key

  • Choose a Key and Scale. The grid only shows notes in that key, so anything you draw fits. Move notes with key moves your patterns into a new key when you change it. All notes shows every note.
  • Draw places a single note, a power chord, a triad or a 7th, using notes from the key.
  • Idea and Generate write a bassline, melody, arpeggio or chord pattern in the key, and Undo brings back the old one.
  • Shift moves the whole pattern a step earlier or later, Scale moves every note a step through the scale, and Oct moves them an octave.
  • Click or drag along the step numbers to choose steps, then Copy and Paste. Velocity and Set change the chosen steps, or the whole pattern.

Mouse and keyboard

Click a stepAdd or remove it. New steps use the Length setting.
Drag from an empty stepDraw one note as long as you drag. Hold Alt for single ticks.
Drag a noteChange where it ends, from its first step or its end marker. It moves in eighths of a step, or hold Alt for single ticks.
Cmd-drag across stepsFill separate steps, or remove them when starting from a filled step.
Right-click a stepEdit velocity, length, timing, probability and sub-steps.
Shift-click a stepCycle velocity: full, 70%, 40%.
SpacePlay and stop, unless you're typing in a text box.
TTurn triplets on or off.
Cmd or Ctrl + ZUndo.
? and EscOpen this help, and close it or the step editor.

Chains, knob movements and files

  • Sub-step, in the step editor, plays a step in quick repeats, where o plays and - is silent.
  • Chain takes pattern numbers such as 1 1 2 3. Tick On and Play goes through them in order, looping.
  • Knobs opens a lane under the grid to draw how a mapped setting moves through the pattern. Alt-drag erases, and Copy all to copies the movements to another pattern.
  • Patterns 1-16 are stored in this browser. Back up saves them with your saved sounds and mappings to one file, and Restore loads it back. Export MIDI and Import MIDI work with one pattern at a time.
  • Sync plays this page together with S-1 Grid and MPC Grid when they're open in the same browser. Play, stop and tempo are shared.
  • Send clock sends MIDI clock with playback. With Sync Source set to Extern in the Protein's settings, its arpeggiator, sequencer and clocked LFOs follow this page's tempo.

If you hear nothing

  • Check that the MIDI badge is green, and reload the page if the Protein was off when it opened.
  • Check the channel, and the Protein's volume.
  • Use Panic if a note gets stuck.
